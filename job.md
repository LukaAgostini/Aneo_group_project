# Group project : Task description
The goal of the group project is to use data to address a meaningful real-world problem or decision. 
You may work with any suitable dataset that was presented in the course, but the project should go beyond simply exploring the data or building a predictive model.

## Your task is to:

* Define a clear problem, opportunity, or decision that could benefit from data.
* Understand the context, stakeholders, objectives, constraints, and success criteria.
* Analyze the available data using suitable data science methods.
* Evaluate the quality of the analysis or model using appropriate technical metrics and baselines.
* Translate the results into business or operational value: what do they mean, what decisions can they support, and what should be done?
* Propose how the analysis/prototype could be turned into a reliable, usable solution, including appropriate testing before deployment.
* Describe how the solution should be monitored and maintained over time.
* The emphasis is not on using the most advanced technique. A strong project demonstrates a clear connection:

**Problem → Data → Analysis → Evidence → Decision/Value → Deployment → Monitoring**

**A dataset alone is not a project**
Your project must identify a meaningful decision, action, or organizational value that can be supported by the analysis.
 
The submission will be a PDF. The suggested format is:
* **Problem definition and business understanding**
    – Describe the context, problem/opportunity, stakeholders, decision to be supported,success criteria and important constraints. (2–3 pages) ​
​
* **Data understanding and background**
    – Present relevant domain background and describe the data, including sources, importantvariables, quality issues, assumptions and limitations. (3–4 pages) ​
​
* **Methods, analysis and technical evaluation**
    – Describe data preparation, methods/models, baselines, validation approach andevaluation metrics. Present and compare the main technical results and limitations. (4–6 pages) ​
​
* **Business evaluation, value and recommendations**
    – Interpret the results in relation to the original problem. Discussbusiness/operational value, relevant KPIs, costs, risks and trade-offs, and provide evidence-based recommendations. (3–5 pages) ​
 
* **Deployment and production readiness**
    – Describe how the analysis/prototype could become a reliable usable solution. Outline theworkflow, required components, testing/validation, and relevant considerations such as reproducibility, versioning, security, scalabilityor staged deployment. (3–5 pages) ​
 
* **Monitoring and maintenance**
    – Describe how the solution should be monitored and maintained. Identify relevant data, solution-performance and business KPIs, possible failures, alerts, responsible stakeholders and appropriate maintenance actions. (2–4 pages) ​

**Page ranges are indicative. Focus on clarity, relevance and evidence rather than length.**
